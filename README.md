# hydrovia

Water is essential for life, yet significant amount of it is wasted According to BPS data in 2023, Indonesia lost 708 million cubic meters of clean water. That is nearly 13% of our total production. It means 1 out of 8 liters water produced is lost before it reaches its destination. As a result, while some areas suffer massive overflow and water losses, other regions face severe water scarcity

Introducing hydrovia. an IoT-based solution designed to solve these exact problems. Hydrovia comes packed with three core features: 
1. Smart Refill : Hydrovia constantly monitors the water level. The moment supplies run low, the system automatically engages the valve to refill the tank 
2. Overflow Prevention : Once the tank reaches its optimal capacity, Hydrovia instantly stops the flow. This eliminates the risk of overflow and wasted water entirely.  
3. Leak Detection System : By comparing input and output flow in real-time, the system can detect anomalies. If a leak is spotted, Hydrovia locks the valve immediately and sends a warning alert to the dashboard.

Hydrovia transforms how we handle water distribution. With hydrovia, No drop wasted and no leak unnoticed. Because water is life, and every drop counts
